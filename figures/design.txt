A methods comparison (N = 100 analysed). The respondents are LLM-generated (Gemini (persona generation; exact version recorded in the run files under data/raw)). Methods compared: Direct persona-conditioned thermometer generation (Bisbee et al. 2024 prompt, updated for 2024), Zaller 8->4: 8 ranked considerations per respondent and target, 4 sampled into each response draw; benchmark: Observed ANES 2024 Time Series pre-election feeling thermometers for the same respondents; metrics: RMSE, MAE, bias, correlation, mean and SD gaps by target, total variation and Wasserstein-1 distance between raw draws and observed ratings, within-respondent draw variation.
